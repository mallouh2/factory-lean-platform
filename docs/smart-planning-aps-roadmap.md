## Future objective: Production Bottleneck Detection and Throughput Improvement

Owner-approved product goal, 24 November 2026. Phase assignment and implementation require separate approval. The existing Group 6, Group 7 and APS gates still apply.

- **Lean continuous improvement.** Use operating evidence such as actual output, run time, queues or WIP, blocking and starvation, changeovers, downtime and scrap where available. Consider product mix, demand and comparable operating conditions before attributing a throughput constraint to a machine, line or stage. Show the evidence, gaps and uncertainty when several constraints are plausible. Carry supported findings through the existing root-cause, corrective-action and effectiveness workflow. Estimate practical gains in good output per unit of time for the affected flow, with assumptions visible. Verify gains against comparable before-and-after operating evidence and check whether the constraint moved elsewhere. Keep estimated gains distinct from measured results.
- **Smart Planning capacity assumptions.** Keep configured rates and theoretical capacity separate from observed feasible throughput by product, line and operating conditions. Evidence-backed estimates may inform future APS proposals as explicit assumptions with their source and uncertainty visible. Weak evidence leaves capacity uncertain. Any future change to an approved capacity standard follows the existing approval rules. An observed constraint alone cannot change an approved plan or turn an estimate into committed capacity.

## Principal risks and required safeguards

| Risk | Required evidence before enabling affected work |
|---|---|
| Combined production diverges from Sales or duplicate accounting occurs | One demand amount per purpose and consistent balances across reservations, authorized execution, corrected Quality, partial Warehouse receipt and dispatch. Test protected conflicts and retries, including two child allocations competing for the same accepted supply. Audit current scalar origin consumers and both legacy and activated receipt paths. |
| A proposal appears feasible from incomplete or inconsistent inputs | Permission-scoped complete snapshot, timestamps and revision checks across calendar, Maintenance, line capabilities, stock and demand. Check side effects of every reused reader. Missing verified mandatory input must surface as unknown or blocked for that check. |
| Approval overwrites a running, pinned or conflicting plan | Reuse protected admission and reasoned overrides. Prove dependent changes revalidate together, preserve revisions, and fail cleanly on stale input. Independent execution events and locks remain authoritative. |
| Configuration mistakes disguise missing transition knowledge or reuse incompatible equipment | Version source and precedence. Model explicit zero, directed rules and overlapping intervals. Preserve mandatory cleaning, safety checks, tool availability and exact product identity even on a zero-duration transition. |
| Opportunity consumes customer buffer, storage or money | Time-based occupancy and capital checks, credible demand and exit dates, explicit agreement for changed commitments, and a safe operating end to the opportunity. New policy cannot retroactively rewrite actual ledgers. |
| Lean claims gain without comparable evidence | Keep the conventional baseline and assumptions, approved revision and actual records independent. Attribute probable improvements only when supported. Missing exposure, supply cost or historical demand remains visible. |

## Future acceptance scenarios

These are synthetic acceptance cases for the authorized implementation phases. Exact values below illustrate repeatable assertions. The suite must capture proposal, authorized outcome and ledger deltas separately.

The initial trial requires the same verified recipe revision. Undated demand ranks after dated orders of the same priority and is flagged for Sales follow-up. |
| S02 · Combined output and short acceptance | Produce a single P run with customer allocations of 40, 35 and 25 units ordered by the approved policy.
